Safe to spendHow much you can spend today after upcoming bills.

The problem

A balance doesn’t know rent is due on the 1st. People have to subtract upcoming bills in their head.

What Cushion does

Every bill and transfer before pay day comes off first. What’s left, spread over the days to pay day, is safe to spend today.

WCAG 2.1 AA

Readable for everyone

Banking apps operating in the EU fall under the European Accessibility Act, which applies WCAG 2.1 AA to mobile apps through EN 301 549. Cushion is designed to that level, together with Apple’s own accessibility guidelines.

  • ContrastText meets 4.5:1, large text and icons 3:1. Forest on lime is 8.3:1, lime on night 11.7:1.
  • Dynamic TypeEvery text style scales with the iPhone text size setting, up to the largest accessibility sizes.
  • Touch targetsButtons and rows are at least 44 × 44 pt.
  • Not colour aloneMoney in and out carries a + or − sign, not only green or red.
  • VoiceOverAmounts are read as full sentences, for example “eighty-eight euros five cents safe to spend”.
  • Reduce MotionCard animations and the coin flip switch off when the setting is on.
