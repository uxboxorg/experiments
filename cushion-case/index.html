Feature 0104 / 12

Safe to spendHow much you can spend today after upcoming bills.

The problem

A balance doesn’t know rent is due on the 1st. People have to subtract upcoming bills in their head.

What Cushion does

Every bill and transfer before pay day comes off first. What’s left, spread over the days to pay day, is safe to spend today.

1 See today’s number
2 Set your comfort line
3 Know when it can’t hold

Bills already counted

Rent, insurance, transfers and the salary on the 22nd are all in the number.

A comfort line you choose

Type the balance you never want to go below. Cushion keeps you above it until pay day.

Ask why

The sparkle on the card asks the advisor to explain today’s number in plain words.

Feature 0205 / 12

The month aheadYour balance day by day until pay day.

The problem

Rent and car insurance are due a day apart, and the account dips lower than expected.

What Cushion does

The month ahead draws your balance day by day to pay day, marks the thinnest moment and lists every bill on its date.

1 Spot the thinnest moment
2 Every bill on its date
3 Pay day at a glance

Thinnest moment

The lowest point before pay day, and how far it sits above your comfort line.

Try a different date

See what happens if car insurance moved to the 6th, before you call anyone.

Add what’s missing

A bill the bank feed missed goes in once and joins the forecast.

Feature 0306 / 12

What Cushion does

Ask in your own words. The advisor answers in plain language, then ends with a card that holds the live numbers and one action.

3 Act on it in the card

Same numbers as Home

Cards you can adjust

Follow-up questions

Each answer suggests the next thing worth asking.

Feature 0407 / 12

Bank approvalCushion prepares transfers and your bank approves them.

The problem

People don’t want a budgeting app to move their money, so suggestions rarely get acted on.

What Cushion does

Cushion fills in the amount, account and reference. Your bank app approves it with Face ID, like any other payment.

1 Cushion suggests it
2 Your bank approves
3 Or start it in chat

Nothing moves on its own

Every transfer starts as a draft you confirm.

Your bank stays in charge

Approval happens in the bank app, with the bank’s own security.

Back where you were

After approving, you return to Cushion with the change already shown.

Design system12 / 12

ComponentsThe six used most across the app.

Every answer card uses the same frame: a title, live numbers, then one main action.

Safe-to-spend card

One number, the days to pay day and the comfort line. The sparkle asks the advisor why.

Answer card

Live numbers you can adjust, with the effect on your line shown as you drag.

Suggestion card

One finding, the reason and a single action. Swipe through them on Home.

Goal card

Progress at a glance, with a cover photo you choose. The photo fades so the text stays readable.

Coming up card

Pay day and the lowest point before it, in plain words.

Draft card

Every action the advisor suggests starts as a draft. Confirm hands it to your bank.
