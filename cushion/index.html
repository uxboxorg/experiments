Cushion, a budget that tells you what's safe to spend

Cushion turns balances, bills and pay day into one number for today, then suggests fixes you can approve with a tap. Every screen reads from the same sample forecast, so a change in one place shows up everywhere. Built for a European launch: euros, European banks and 24-hour time.

Things to try

  • Approve “Move €1,200 into savings”, then check The month ahead
  • Open “Cancel the second Spotify” and cancel it on Spotify’s site
  • In Fixes, email Q-Park about the duplicate parking charge, then put the refund towards Japan
  • Move car insurance to the 6th in The month ahead
  • Ask Chat “Can I afford a €600 flight in March?” or try the voice demo
  • Open the Accounts tab to add a sample N26 account or swipe one away
  • Sign out from your profile to try logging in and creating an account
