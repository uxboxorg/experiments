Cushion, a budget that tells you what's safe to spend

Cushion turns balances, bills and pay day into one number for today, then suggests fixes you can approve with a tap. Every screen reads from the same sample forecast, so a change in one place shows up everywhere.

Things to try

  • Approve “Move $1,200 into savings”, then check The month ahead
  • Open “Cancel the second Spotify” and let Cushion handle it
  • Send the fixes money to the Iceland trip, then change the monthly amount
  • Move the car payment to the 6th in The month ahead
  • Ask Chat “Can I afford a $600 flight in March?” or try the voice demo
  • Tap the gear to add a sample Chase account
